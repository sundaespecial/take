Where the words live

Plaintext exists in exactly two places. The handset, and whatever tenant the firm already trusts. Everything in between is opaque by construction, not by policy. This prototype implements the handset and the desk on one page; the relay hop is simulated by the sealed blob you can export and import on the Desk screen.
