Where the words live

Plaintext exists in exactly two places. The handset, and whatever tenant the firm already trusts. Everything in between is opaque by construction, not by policy. This prototype implements the handset; sharing between devices is an ephemeral link carrying a sealed or plainly-encoded payload in the URL fragment — there is no relay server.
